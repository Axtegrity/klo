Getting into your admin panel

How to log in and find your way around
1
Go to your admin page

Open your browser and go to app.keithlodom.io/admin (or klo-app.vercel.app/admin if you are using the preview link).

2
Sign in with your admin account

Enter your admin email and password on the sign-in page. This is the account Tim set up for you — not a public account.

3
You will land on the Admin Dashboard

You will see a dark screen with stats at the top and a row of tabs across the middle. The tabs are: Overview, Creative Studio, Customize, Content, and more. You will live mostly in the Content tab.

💡
Tip: Bookmark the admin page You can bookmark /admin in your browser so you do not have to type it every time.

Adding and editing vault articles

Your Vault Library is where all your written content lives
Admin Dashboard › Content tab › Vault Library

The Content tab has four sections inside it: Home Page, Vault Library, Feed Posts, and Repository. Click Vault Library to manage your articles.

To add a brand-new article:

1
Click the blue "New Item" button

You will find this button in the top-right area of the Vault Library section.

2
A form pops up — fill in the Title

Type the name of your article in the Title field. This is the headline everyone sees. Make it clear and direct.

3
Choose a Category and Type

Pick a Category from the dropdown (for example: Leadership, Governance, AI & Ethics). Then pick a Type — most articles are simply article. Videos use video, and so on.

4
Write your content in the Body field

The big text area labeled Body is where your full article goes. Paste from a document or type directly. No special formatting needed — plain text is fine.

5
Click "Create"

The blue Create button saves your new article. It will go live immediately as a published item.

🔎
New articles are free-tier by default When you create a new article it is visible to all users. After you create it, you can open it to edit and change the Tier to restrict it to paying members.

To edit an existing article:

1
Find the article in the list

Scroll through your vault items or use the search bar at the top — type part of the title and the list will filter automatically.

2
Click the pencil icon (✎)

Every article row has a small pencil icon on the right side. Click it to open the edit form for that article.

3
Make your changes

Update any field — title, body, category, author, published date, tier, or anything else. You can change as many fields as you want in one go.

4
Click the blue "Save" button

Your changes go live as soon as you click Save. You will see the button change to Saving... for a moment, then the form closes. Done.

✍️
The Excerpt field is optional but helpful If you fill in the Excerpt field, that short summary appears in the card preview on the site. If you leave it blank, the app automatically uses the first few lines of your body text.

What each field means:

Field What it does
Title The headline of the article — shown everywhere it appears
Category Groups the article so members can filter by topic
Type Labels what kind of content this is (article, video, guide, etc.)
Tier Controls who can read it: free = everyone, others = paying members only
Excerpt A short 1–2 sentence preview shown in the card list
Body The full article text
Author The name that appears at the bottom of the article
Published Date The date shown on the article — you can backdate it if needed

Featuring an article in the Executive Feed

Put your best content front and center on the public /feed page

The Executive Intelligence Feed is the main public-facing feed on your site. Only vault articles you specifically flag will appear there — you are in full control.

Admin Dashboard › Content tab › Vault Library › click the ✎ pencil on any article
1
Open the article you want to feature

Find the article in your Vault Library and click its pencil icon to open the edit form.

2
Scroll to the bottom of the edit form

Near the bottom you will see a checkbox labeled Feature in Executive Feed.

3
Check the box to turn it on

Click the checkbox. A small checkmark will appear. Underneath it you will see the note: "Surfaces this article on the public /feed page."

4
Click Save

That is it. The article will now show up in your Executive Feed. Visitors to your site will see it on the feed page.

💡
To remove an article from the feed Open the same article again, uncheck the Feature in Executive Feed box, and click Save. The article stays in your vault — it just comes off the public feed.
📋
There is no limit on how many articles you can feature You can feature as many or as few as you want. Recommended: pick your strongest 3–6 pieces so the feed stays sharp and focused.

Managing categories

Keep your content organized so members can find what they need

Categories are the labels that group your vault articles. Your current categories are:

AI & Ethics Church & Tech Governance Leadership Youth & Workforce Previous Events Current Events

To assign a category to an article:

1
Open any article in edit mode

Click the pencil icon next to the article in your Vault Library list.

2
Click the "Category" dropdown

You will see a dropdown menu with all your available categories. Click it to open the list.

3
Pick the right category and click Save

Choose the one that best fits the article topic. One article belongs to one category. Click Save to apply it.

🔍
Want to add a new category? Categories are managed in the app settings by Tim. Send Tim a quick note with the category name you want to add and he can get it in the list for you. This keeps things clean and consistent.

Browsing by category in the admin panel:

You can see how many articles are in each category right on the admin Analytics tab. Go to the Analytics tab and look for Vault Content by Type. It shows a breakdown so you can spot any gaps.

Publishing or hiding content

Control exactly what members see — and when

Every vault article has a visibility state. Think of it like a light switch with three settings:

State What it means
Published Visible to members. This is the normal "live" state.
Hidden Not visible to members, but not deleted. You can un-hide it anytime.
Archived Removed from the main list but still stored. Still used by KLO Intelligence. Can be republished.

To change an article's visibility:

1
Find the article in your Vault Library list

Use the search bar if you need to narrow it down.

2
Look for the visibility toggle on the right side of the article card

Each article row shows a small toggle control. It will say the current state: Published or Hidden.

3
Click the toggle to switch states

Click it once to change. The change saves automatically — you do not need to click a separate Save button for visibility.

⚠️
Hiding is not the same as deleting If you hide an article, it disappears from the member view — but it is still in your vault. You can make it visible again anytime. Deleting is permanent and cannot be undone. When in doubt, hide first.

To filter by published vs. hidden:

1
Look at the toggle bar near the search box

You will see two buttons: published and hidden.

2
Click "hidden" to see all your hidden articles

This shows only the articles you have turned off. Useful for reviewing what is sitting in draft mode.

3
Click "published" to go back to the live view

This shows everything that is currently visible to your members.

📦
Archived items are at the bottom At the very bottom of your Vault Library you will see a gold-colored row labeled Archived Items. Click it to expand and see everything you have archived. You can republish any of them with one click.

FAQ

Common questions and "wait — what just happened?" moments
I clicked Save but I do not see my changes on the site.
Give it 5–10 seconds and then do a hard refresh on your browser (hold Shift and click the reload button, or press Ctrl + Shift + R on Windows / Cmd + Shift + R on Mac). Your changes are saved — sometimes the browser shows you a cached version of the old page.
I hid an article by accident. How do I get it back?
No problem. Click the hidden filter button above the vault list to see all your hidden articles. Find the article and click its visibility toggle to set it back to Published. Done.
I deleted an article by accident. Can I get it back?
Unfortunately, a permanent delete cannot be reversed from the admin panel. Contact Tim as soon as possible — he may be able to recover it from a recent database backup if you catch it quickly.
An article shows "Premium" on the site. What does that mean?
The article has its Tier set to something other than free. That means only paying members at that tier can read the full content. To make it available to everyone, open the article, change the Tier dropdown to free, and click Save.
I turned on "Feature in Executive Feed" but the article is not showing up on the feed page.
Check two things: (1) Make sure the article is set to Published — a hidden article will not appear in the feed even if the toggle is on. (2) Do a hard refresh on the feed page (Shift + reload). If it is still missing after that, reach out to Tim.
What is the difference between "Vault Library" and "Feed Posts"?
Vault articles are your full-length written content — guides, briefings, leadership pieces. Feed posts are shorter community-style updates like announcements and discussions. The Executive Feed on the public site pulls from vault articles that have the feature flag turned on — not from feed posts.
What does "Archived" mean — is the content deleted?
No. Archived content is stored safely and still used by KLO Intelligence (the AI that powers your advisor). It is just removed from the active member-facing view. Think of it as a back room — the content is there, members just cannot browse to it. You can republish it anytime from the Archived section at the bottom of the vault list.
Can I add a new category myself?
Not from the admin panel — categories are set in the code by Tim. If you want a new one, just text or email Tim with the name and he will add it. Usually takes less than a day.
I see a "Request Update" button at the top of the admin dashboard. What is that?
That button is a direct line to Tim to request a feature or change in the app itself. Use it anytime you want something added or adjusted — Tim will see it and get back to you.
Is it safe to explore the admin panel? Will I break anything?
Yes, it is safe to explore. The only actions that make permanent changes are deleting content and resetting assessment results (which is in the Tools tab under "Danger Zone"). Everything else — editing, hiding, featuring — is fully reversible. Click around with confidence.
