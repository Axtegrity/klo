# Tutorial 04: Adding a New Item to the Vault Library
**Duration estimate:** 10 minutes
**Series:** KLO Admin Video Tutorials

---

## Prerequisites

Before you start this tutorial, you need:
- Admin access (see Tutorial 01)
- The content you want to add — a title, the body text or article content, a category, and a content type
- Optionally: a thumbnail image URL and the tier you want to gate the content behind

---

## What You Will Learn

By the end of this video, you will know how to open the Vault Library, create a new vault item, fill in all the required fields, choose who can see it, and save it so it appears in the vault for your members.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1 — Open the Content tab and select Vault Library

1. Click the **Content** tab in the tab row.
2. Look at the four section buttons: **Home Page**, **Vault Library**, **Feed Posts**, and **Repository**.
3. Click **Vault Library**.

### Step 2 — Review the vault toolbar

At the top of the Vault Library section you will see a toolbar with four controls:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Search box** | Filters the vault list as you type |
| **Published / Hidden** toggle | Switches the list between showing live items and hidden items |
| **New Item** button | Opens the form to create a brand new vault item |
| **Refresh icon** (circular arrow) | Reloads the list from the database |

[SHOW: the toolbar with all four controls labeled]

---

### Step 3 — Click New Item

1. Click the **New Item** button in the toolbar.

A creation form will slide open below the toolbar.

[SHOW: the New Item form opening]
[NARRATE: "This form is long — scroll down inside it to see all the fields. I will walk through each one."]

### Step 4 — Fill in the required fields

Work through the form from top to bottom.

**Title** (required)
- Type the full title of the content.
- Example: "The AI Executive Order: What Leaders Need to Know"

**Slug** (required)
- The slug is the URL-friendly version of the title. It is auto-generated from the title — lowercase, no spaces, hyphens between words.
- Example: `the-ai-executive-order-what-leaders-need-to-know`
- You can edit the slug if needed, but keep it lowercase with only letters, numbers, and hyphens.

[SHOW: the Title and Slug fields with an example filled in]
[NARRATE: "The slug becomes part of the URL that vault items use. Once you publish an item and share its link, changing the slug will break that link. Choose the slug carefully."]

**Content Type** (required)
- Click the **Content Type** dropdown and choose one:
  - article
  - video
  - template
  - guide
  - briefing
  - framework
  - policy
  - replay
  - event

**Category** (required)
- Click the **Category** dropdown and choose one:
  - AI & Ethics
  - Church & Tech
  - Governance
  - Leadership
  - Youth & Workforce
  - Previous Events
  - Current Events

[SHOW: both dropdowns with the full option lists visible]
[PAUSE: let them read the options]

**Tier Required**
- Choose who can access this item:
  - **free** — anyone with an account, including free members
  - **essentials** — essentials subscribers and above
  - **professional** — professional subscribers and above
  - **enterprise** — enterprise subscribers only

[SHOW: the Tier Required dropdown]
[NARRATE: "If you want to make an item available to everyone as a preview or lead magnet, set it to free. For premium content, set it to professional or enterprise."]

---

### Step 5 — Fill in the body content

1. Find the **Body** field — it is a large text area.
2. Paste or type the full article or content text here.
3. Basic formatting is supported. Long articles may use paragraph breaks.

**Excerpt** (optional but recommended)
- The excerpt is the short preview text shown on the vault card before the member clicks in.
- Aim for two to three sentences — about 200 to 400 characters.
- If you leave it blank, the system will show nothing in the preview.

[SHOW: the Body and Excerpt fields]
[NARRATE: "The excerpt is what makes members want to click. Write it like a headline teaser — what will they learn, and why does it matter to them specifically?"]

---

### Step 6 — Add optional fields

**Thumbnail URL** (optional)
- If you have an image URL for the card thumbnail, paste it here.
- Leave blank if you do not have one — the vault will show a placeholder.

**Author Name** (optional)
- If the content has a byline other than Keith, type the author's name.
- Leave blank to show no author credit.

**Published At** (optional)
- This is the display date shown on the card.
- If you leave it blank, the system uses today's date.
- Format: YYYY-MM-DD, for example 2026-06-01.

[SHOW: the optional fields area]

---

### Step 7 — Save the new item

1. Scroll to the bottom of the form.
2. Click the **Save** button.

The form will close and the new item will appear at the top of the Vault Library list.

[SHOW: the new item appearing in the list]
[NARRATE: "By default, new items are published immediately when you save them. If you want to hide an item while you finish writing it, you can toggle its visibility after saving — that is covered in Tutorial 05."]

---

### Step 8 — Verify the item in the vault

1. Open a new browser tab.
2. Go to **keithlodom.ai/vault** and sign in as a member.
3. Find the new item in the list and click it to confirm it opens correctly.

[SHOW: the vault page with the new item visible]
[NARRATE: "Always do a quick check on the live vault. Confirm the title, excerpt, and content look right from a member's point of view."]

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: I see an error that says "Slug already exists."**
Fix: Another item in the vault already uses that slug. Change the slug field to something slightly different — for example, add a year or a number at the end.

**Problem: The item saved but I cannot find it in the vault on the live site.**
Fix: Check the Published / Hidden toggle in the toolbar. If you accidentally saved it as hidden, switch to Hidden view, find the item, and change its visibility to Published.

**Problem: The thumbnail is not showing.**
Fix: The URL must be a direct link to an image file ending in .jpg, .png, or .webp. Check that the URL actually loads an image in a browser tab by itself.

**Problem: A member says they cannot see the item even though they are subscribed.**
Fix: Check the Tier Required field on the item. The member's subscription tier must match or exceed the required tier.
