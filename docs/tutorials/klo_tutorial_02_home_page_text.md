# Tutorial 02: Editing Your Home Page Text
**Duration estimate:** 10 minutes
**Series:** KLO Admin Video Tutorials

---

## Prerequisites

Before you start this tutorial, you need:
- Admin access (see Tutorial 01 if you have not signed in yet)
- The admin dashboard open at keithlodom.ai/admin

---

## What You Will Learn

By the end of this video, you will know how to edit the words on every section of the home page — the hero headline, tagline, intelligence brief, trending topics, featured insight, and more — and publish those changes so they appear live on the site.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1 — Open the Content tab

1. Click the **Content** tab in the tab row at the top of the admin dashboard.

### Step 2 — Make sure you are on Home Page

1. Inside the Content tab, look at the four section buttons near the top: **Home Page**, **Vault Library**, **Feed Posts**, and **Repository**.
2. Click **Home Page**.

### Step 3 — Read the section list

You will see a list of editable sections. Each one is a row with a label, a short description, and a pencil icon on the right. The sections are:

|---|---|
| **Hero Banner** | The main heading ("Keith L. Odom"), the label above it, and the tagline |
| **Latest Intelligence Brief** | The featured article card — title, date, excerpt, and link |
| **Trending Topics** | The topic tags and the section heading |
| **Featured Insight** | The highlighted article card — category, title, description, and link |
| **AI Tool of the Week** | The weekly AI tool spotlight |
| **Survey CTA** | The survey callout section |

### Step 4 — Edit the Hero Banner

The Hero Banner is the very first thing visitors see when they land on your site.

1. Find the **Hero Banner** row.
2. Click the pencil icon on the right side of that row.

[SHOW: the Hero Banner row with the pencil icon]
[NARRATE: "The pencil icon opens an edit panel for that section. Every section has one."]

3. An edit panel will open. You will see three fields:

| Field | What it changes |
|---|---|
| **Label (above name)** | The small text above the main heading — for example, "Technology Innovator · Strategic Advisor · Speaker" |
| **Main Heading** | The large name display — "Keith L. Odom" |
| **Tagline** | The sentence below the heading that describes the mission |

4. Click inside the **Label** field and change the text as needed.
5. Click inside the **Tagline** field and update it.
6. Do not change **Main Heading** unless you have been specifically told to.

[SHOW: the edit panel with the three fields visible]
[NARRATE: "The Main Heading is the name — you almost certainly want to leave that alone. The tagline is the most common edit. Keep it under two sentences."]

7. When finished, click the **Save** button at the bottom of the panel.

[SHOW: the Save button]
[NARRATE: "Save writes your changes to the database immediately. The next step is to publish — save and publish are two separate actions here."]

---

### Step 5 — Edit the Latest Intelligence Brief

The Latest Intelligence Brief is the featured article card on the home page. When you have new content in the vault that you want to spotlight, update this section.

1. Find the **Latest Intelligence Brief** row.
2. Click the pencil icon.
3. You will see five fields:

| Field | What it changes |
|---|---|
| **Brief Title** | The title of the featured article |
| **Date** | The date shown on the card |
| **Excerpt** | The short summary paragraph |
| **Read More Link** | The URL the button links to — usually a vault item path like /vault/your-article-slug |
| **Button Label** | The text on the button — default is "Read More" |

4. Update any fields that need changing.
5. Click **Save**.

[SHOW: the edit panel with the five fields]
[NARRATE: "The Read More Link needs to match a real article in your vault. If you type a path that does not exist, the link will go to a 404 page. Double-check the slug from your Vault Library."]

---

### Step 6 — Edit the Trending Topics section

The Trending Topics section shows a row of clickable topic tags. Update them whenever the conversation in your industry shifts.

1. Find the **Trending Topics** row.
2. Click the pencil icon.
3. You will see:

| Field | What it changes |
|---|---|
| **Week of / Date** | The date label shown above the tags — for example, "Week of June 2, 2026" |
| **Section Heading** | The larger label — for example, "Trending in Tech & Faith" |
| **Topic 1 through Topic 5** | Each tag label |

4. Update the date and any topics that need refreshing.
5. Click **Save**.

[SHOW: the edit panel with the topic fields]
[NARRATE: "Keep topic labels short — two or three words. Anything longer gets cut off on mobile screens."]

---

### Step 7 — Review and verify the other sections

Repeat the same pencil → edit → Save process for any other section you want to update:

- **Featured Insight** — update whenever you want a different vault article in the spotlight
- **AI Tool of the Week** — update weekly with a new AI recommendation
- **Survey CTA** — update if you want to point to a different survey

[SHOW: each section row briefly as it is named]
[NARRATE: "You do not need to update every section every time. Just open the ones that need changes."]

---

### Step 8 — Publish your changes

Saving a field stores your changes in the database. Publishing makes them visible on the live website.

1. Look at the top of the Content tab for a **Publish** button, or return to the Overview tab.
2. If prompted to publish, click **Publish**.
3. Open a new browser tab and go to **keithlodom.ai** to confirm the changes appear.

[SHOW: the live home page after the update]
[NARRATE: "Always verify on the live site after publishing. What you see in the admin panel is a text form — the live page is what your members actually see."]

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: My changes saved but nothing changed on the live site.**
Fix: Check whether you clicked Save inside the edit panel AND then published. Both steps are required. Refresh keithlodom.ai to clear any browser cache.

**Problem: The Read More link on the Intelligence Brief goes to a 404 page.**
Fix: The link must match the exact slug of a vault item. Go to the Content tab, click Vault Library, find the article, and copy its slug exactly.

**Problem: A topic tag looks cut off on mobile.**
Fix: Keep topic labels to three words or fewer. Edit the Trending Topics section and shorten the label.

**Problem: I cannot find the pencil icon for a section.**
Fix: Some sections may scroll off screen. Scroll down inside the Home Page view to find all section rows.
