# Tutorial 05: Editing, Hiding, and Archiving Vault Items
**Duration estimate:** 8 minutes
**Series:** KLO Admin Video Tutorials

---

## Prerequisites

Before you start this tutorial, you need:
- Admin access (see Tutorial 01)
- At least one vault item already created (see Tutorial 04)
- The admin dashboard open at keithlodom.ai/admin

---

## What You Will Learn

By the end of this video, you will know how to find an existing vault item, edit its content, hide it from members without deleting it, and archive it when it is no longer current — while keeping it accessible to the KLO Intelligence system.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1 — Open the Vault Library

1. Click the **Content** tab in the tab row.
2. Click **Vault Library** from the four section buttons.

[SHOW: the Content tab with Vault Library selected]

---

### Step 2 — Find the item you want to manage

The vault list shows all published items by default. Each item shows its title, content type, category, and tier badge.

**To search:**
1. Click in the search box at the top of the toolbar.
2. Type part of the item's title.
3. The list filters as you type.

**To see hidden items:**
1. Find the **Published / Hidden** toggle in the toolbar.
2. Click **Hidden** to switch the view.
3. Hidden items appear with a muted style.

[SHOW: the search box being used and the Published/Hidden toggle]
[NARRATE: "Published and Hidden are two separate views. If you cannot find an item in the Published view, check the Hidden view — it may have been hidden previously."]

---

### Step 3 — Edit a vault item

1. Find the item in the list.
2. Look for the pencil icon on the right side of the item row.
3. Click the pencil icon.

An inline edit form will open below the item row.

[SHOW: the pencil icon on an item row and the edit form opening]

4. Update any fields you need to change — title, body, excerpt, category, tier, or any other field.
5. When finished, click the **Save** button at the bottom of the form.

[SHOW: the Save button in the edit form]
[NARRATE: "Saving an edit updates the content immediately. Members who reload the vault page will see the updated version right away — there is no separate publish step for edits."]

---

### Step 4 — Feature an item (star it)

If you want to pin a vault item to the top of the list for members, you can star it.

1. Find the item in the list.
2. Look for the star icon next to the pencil icon.
3. Click the star icon.
4. A filled gold star means the item is featured and will appear at or near the top of the vault for members.
5. Click the star again to un-feature it.

[SHOW: the star icon being toggled from empty to gold]
[NARRATE: "Featured items get prominent placement. Use this sparingly — having ten featured items defeats the purpose. One to three is ideal."]

---

### Step 5 — Hide an item

Hiding removes the item from what members can see, but keeps it in your system. Use this for:
- Draft content you are not ready to publish
- Seasonal content you want to bring back later
- Items being revised

1. Find the item in the Published view.
2. Look for the eye icon (visibility toggle) on the item row.
3. Click the eye icon.
4. The item will disappear from the Published list.
5. Switch to the **Hidden** view to confirm it is there.

[SHOW: the eye icon being clicked and the item disappearing from the list]
[NARRATE: "Hidden items still exist in the database — they just do not show up for members. This is reversible. To bring a hidden item back, switch to the Hidden view and click the eye icon again."]

**To unhide an item:**
1. Click **Hidden** in the Published / Hidden toggle.
2. Find the item.
3. Click the eye icon again — it will return to the Published list.

---

### Step 6 — Archive an item

Archiving is for content that is no longer current but should stay in the system because the KLO Intelligence AI may reference it when answering member questions.

1. Find the item in the vault list.
2. Look for the archive icon (a box with a down-arrow) next to the other icons.
3. Click the archive icon.
4. A confirmation prompt may appear — confirm the action.

The item will move to the **Repository** section of the Content tab.

[SHOW: the archive icon and the item moving to Repository]
[NARRATE: "Archiving is one step further than hiding. Hidden items can be easily restored. Archived items live in the Repository — still searchable by your AI system, but clearly flagged as archived so members do not see them."]

**To view archived items:**
1. Stay in the Content tab.
2. Click the **Repository** section button.
3. All archived content is listed there.

---

### Step 7 — Delete an item permanently

Use deletion only when content is completely wrong, duplicated, or must not exist in the system at all. Deletion cannot be undone.

1. Find the item.
2. Look for the trash icon on the item row.
3. Click the trash icon.
4. A confirmation dialog will appear: "Are you sure you want to delete this item?"
5. Click **Delete** to confirm, or **Cancel** to go back.

[SHOW: the delete confirmation dialog]
[NARRATE: "Delete is permanent and irreversible. If in doubt, hide or archive instead — those give you a way back."]

---

## Quick Reference: When to use each action

| Action | When to use it |
|---|---|
| **Edit** | Update text, tier, category, or any field |
| **Star / Feature** | Pin an important item to the top of the member vault |
| **Hide** | Temporarily remove from member view — reversible |
| **Archive** | Retire content while keeping it for AI reference |
| **Delete** | Remove permanently — use rarely |

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: I cannot find the item in either Published or Hidden view.**
Fix: Try clearing the search box — if there is text in it, the list is filtered. Also check the Repository view in case it was archived.

**Problem: I clicked the eye icon but the item is still visible on the live vault.**
Fix: Ask your browser to do a hard refresh on the vault page (Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows, Cmd+Shift+R on Mac). The change is instant in the database but browsers cache the page.

**Problem: The trash icon is not visible on the item row.**
Fix: On narrow screens, action icons may be hidden behind a collapsed menu. Try on a wider window, or scroll the item row to the right.
