# Tutorial 06: Creating and Publishing an Event
**Duration estimate:** 12 minutes
**Series:** KLO Admin Video Tutorials

---

## Prerequisites

Before you start this tutorial, you need:
- Admin access (see Tutorial 01)
- The event details ready: conference name, location, date, time, and a description
- Optionally: a website URL, any supporting files (PDF, PowerPoint, Word), and an access code if the event requires one

---

## What You Will Learn

By the end of this video, you will know how to create a new event, fill in all its details, upload supporting files, publish it to the events page, feature it on the home page, and control how the event spotlight and countdown appear to visitors.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1 — Open the Events tab

1. Click the **Events** tab in the tab row at the top of the admin dashboard.

[SHOW: the tab row with Events highlighted]
[NARRATE: "The Events tab manages everything on the public /events page — upcoming appearances, conference details, and downloadable files for attendees."]

---

### Step 2 — Read the Events page layout

The Events tab has two main sections:

1. **Countdown & Spotlight Controls** — at the top, with a blue border. This controls what appears on the public events page header.
2. **Event Presentations** — below that, where your individual events live.

We will start by creating an event, then come back to configure the spotlight at the end.

[SHOW: the two main sections of the Events tab]

---

### Step 3 — Click Add Event

1. Scroll down past the Spotlight section to the **Event Presentations** heading.
2. Look for the **Add Event** button in the top-right corner of that section.
3. Click **Add Event**.

A form panel will appear below the button.

[SHOW: the Add Event button and the form opening]

---

### Step 4 — Optional: Upload a document to auto-fill

If you have a conference program or agenda as a PDF, Word doc, or text file, you can let the system read it and fill in the event details automatically.

1. Inside the form, look for the document upload zone at the top — it shows an upload icon and says "Upload a document to auto-fill."
2. Click the upload zone.
3. Select your document file (PDF, DOC, DOCX, or TXT).
4. Wait a moment — the system will extract event details and show them for your review.
5. Review each extracted field, make any corrections, and click **Create Event** next to each entry.

[SHOW: the document upload zone and an extracted event appearing]
[NARRATE: "The AI reads the document and pulls out conference names, locations, and dates. It is not perfect — always review what it extracts before clicking Create Event. You can edit any field right in the review card."]

If you prefer to fill the form manually, skip the document upload and proceed to Step 5.

---

### Step 5 — Fill in the manual form

Work through each field:

**Conference Name** (required)
- The full name of the conference, event, or engagement.
- Example: "National Church Leaders Summit 2026"

**Location** (required)
- City and state, or venue name and city.
- Example: "Atlanta, GA" or "Marriott Marquis, Atlanta, GA"

**Date** (required)
- Click the date field and choose the date from the calendar picker.
- If the date is not confirmed yet, click the **TBD** button next to the date field. This will display "SAVE THE DATE" on the events page instead of a specific date.

[SHOW: the date field with the calendar picker and the TBD button]
[NARRATE: "The TBD button is for events you know are coming but do not have a date for yet. Members will see 'Save the Date' instead of a blank."]

**Conference Start Date and Conference End Date** (optional)
- If the conference spans multiple days, fill in both start and end dates.
- The system will display a range on the spotlight card — for example, "June 14–16, 2026."

**Time** (optional)
- Keith's specific start time for his session.

**Timezone**
- Click the dropdown and choose the correct timezone:
  - Eastern (ET)
  - Central (CT)
  - Mountain (MT)
  - Pacific (PT)
  - Arizona (MST)
  - Alaska (AKT)
  - Hawaii (HST)

**Category**
- Choose **Current Events** for upcoming appearances.
- Choose **Previous Events** for past appearances.

[SHOW: the timezone and category dropdowns]

**Description** (optional)
- A paragraph describing the event or Keith's role at it.
- This appears in the expanded event view on the events page.

**Notes** (optional)
- Additional details shown when a visitor clicks "View More Details."
- Use this for parking information, dress code, registration links, or any logistical notes.

**Website URL** (optional)
- The official conference website. Paste the full URL including https://.

---

### Step 6 — Guest Presenter toggle

If Keith is presenting at another organization's conference — rather than hosting his own — turn on the Guest Presenter toggle.

1. Find the **Guest Presenter** toggle (it says "Keith is presenting at another conference").
2. Click the toggle to turn it on (it will turn blue).
3. Two additional fields will appear:
   - **Session Name** — the specific session or talk title
   - **Room Location** — the room or hall where Keith will speak (optional)

[SHOW: the Guest Presenter toggle turning on and the extra fields appearing]
[NARRATE: "When Guest Presenter is on, the event card on the public page will show the session name and room, so attendees at a large conference can find Keith easily."]

---

### Step 7 — Create the event

1. Scroll to the bottom of the form.
2. Click **Create Event**.

The form will close and your new event will appear in the event list under **Current Events**.

[SHOW: the new event appearing in the Current Events list]

---

### Step 8 — Publish the event

Creating an event saves it to the database, but it starts as a draft. Publishing makes it visible on the public /events page.

1. Find your new event in the list.
2. Look at the right side of the event row — you will see a globe icon.
3. Click the globe icon.
4. The icon will turn green and a **Published** badge will appear on the event row.

[SHOW: the globe icon turning green with the Published badge appearing]
[NARRATE: "An unpublished event exists in your admin panel but is invisible to the public. Always publish after creating an event — otherwise visitors will not see it."]

---

### Step 9 — Upload supporting files

If you have agenda PDFs, slide decks, or other documents to make available for this event:

1. Click anywhere on the event row to expand it.
2. Scroll down to the **Files** section at the bottom of the expanded view.
3. Click **Upload File**.
4. Select your file (PDF, DOC, DOCX, PPT, PPTX, XLS, XLSX, or TXT — maximum 50 MB per file).
5. Wait for the upload confirmation message to appear.

[SHOW: the Files section and the Upload File button]
[NARRATE: "Uploaded files become downloadable from the event's detail page. Members attending a conference can download the agenda or slides right from their phone."]

---

### Step 10 — Feature the event on the home page (optional)

If you want this event to appear on the home page as the featured upcoming keynote:

1. Click the star icon on the event row.
2. The star will turn gold — this means the event is featured and will display in the "Upcoming Keynote" section on the home page.
3. Only one event can be featured at a time. Featuring a new event will automatically un-feature the previous one.

[SHOW: the star icon turning gold]

---

### Step 11 — Configure the Countdown and Spotlight

Go back to the top of the Events tab to configure the public /events page header.

1. Scroll up to the **Countdown & Spotlight Controls** section (blue border).

The key settings are:

| Toggle | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Show Countdown Timer** | When on, a numeric countdown clock appears above the spotlight card |
| **Auto-Pick Next Event** | When on, the spotlight automatically shows the nearest upcoming event |
| **Show Live Events Section** | When on, live events appear in their own section on /events |
| **Show Upcoming Events Section** | When on, upcoming events appear in their own section |
| **Show Past Events Section** | When on, past events appear in their own section |

2. Adjust the toggles as needed.
3. Click **Save Spotlight Settings**.

[SHOW: the Countdown & Spotlight section with toggles]
[NARRATE: "The most important one here is Auto-Pick. Leave it on and the countdown and spotlight will automatically update to show whichever event is coming up next — you do not have to touch it every time. Turn it off only if you want to manually pin a specific event."]

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: The event is not appearing on the /events page.**
Fix: Check that you clicked the globe icon to publish the event. Also confirm that "Display on Events Page" is toggled on inside the event's edit form.

**Problem: The countdown is showing the wrong event.**
Fix: Make sure Auto-Pick is on in the Spotlight section, and click Save Spotlight Settings. The auto-pick always chooses the nearest future event by date.

**Problem: A file upload failed.**
Fix: Check the file size (maximum 50 MB) and the file type (PDF, DOC, DOCX, PPT, PPTX, XLS, XLSX, TXT only). Other formats will be rejected.

**Problem: The TBD button is not showing.**
Fix: The TBD button appears to the right of the date field in the creation form and in the edit form. If it is not visible, the form may be scrolled or the window may be too narrow — try widening your browser window.
