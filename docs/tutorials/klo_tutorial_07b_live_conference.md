# Tutorial 07b: Running a Live Conference
**Duration estimate:** 10 minutes
**Series:** KLO Admin Video Tutorials

---

## Prerequisites

Before you start this tutorial, you need:
- Admin access (see Tutorial 01)
- An event already created and published (see Tutorial 06)
- Sessions already added to the event (see Tutorial 07a)
- The admin dashboard open on a phone or tablet you can keep with you during the event — or a laptop at the presenter table

---

## What You Will Learn

By the end of this video, you will know how to turn an event live so the audience can engage, launch and manage polls in real time, moderate Q&A questions, push announcements to all attendees, activate the Word Cloud, and export engagement data after the event.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1 — Open the Conference tab and find your event

1. Click the **Conference** tab in the tab row.
2. Find your event in the list.

[SHOW: the Conference tab and the event list]

---

### Step 2 — Turn the event ON (go live)

Each event card in the Conference list has a toggle switch on the left side. This is the event's live toggle.

1. Find the toggle on the left side of your event's card — it is a small pill-shaped switch.
2. Click the toggle.
3. The toggle will turn green.
4. The event card will show a **LIVE** badge.
5. The engagement status bar at the top of the Conference tab will show "Engagement ON" with a pulsing green dot.

[SHOW: the toggle turning green and the LIVE badge appearing]
[NARRATE: "The moment you flip this toggle, the engagement features become visible to your audience on the live event page. They can now see the Q&A box, submit to the Word Cloud, and see any polls you deploy. Nothing goes to them automatically — you control what appears."]

---

### Step 3 — Navigate into the event

1. Click on the event name or the arrow icon on the event card to enter the event detail view.
2. You will see the six sub-tabs: **Sessions**, **Polls**, **Q&A**, **Word Cloud**, **Announce**, **Settings**.

[SHOW: the event detail view with the six sub-tabs]

---

### Step 4 — Running Polls

Polls are questions you push to the audience and they answer in real time. The results appear as live bars on your screen — and optionally on a display screen at the venue.

**To create a poll:**
1. Click the **Polls** sub-tab.
2. Click the **Create Poll** or **New Poll** button.
3. Type your question.
4. Add answer options (at least two, up to as many as you need).
5. Save the poll — it is now in your draft list, not yet visible to the audience.

[SHOW: the poll creation form with a question and options filled in]

**To deploy a poll (make it live for the audience):**
1. Find your poll in the Polls list.
2. Click the **Deploy** button next to the poll.
3. The poll goes live immediately. The audience will see it on their screens.
4. Watch the live results appear as bars that fill in as people vote.

[SHOW: deploying a poll and watching results populate]
[NARRATE: "Polls are powerful for audience participation. Ask a question, let the room answer, then discuss the results together. The bars update in real time — no refreshing needed."]

**To close a poll:**
1. Click the **Close** or **Deactivate** button on the active poll.
2. The audience can no longer vote. The results stay visible on your screen for reference.

---

### Step 5 — Moderating Q&A

The Q&A feature lets audience members submit questions from their phones. You see every question before the audience does — you choose which ones to show.

1. Click the **Q&A** sub-tab.
2. As audience members submit questions, they appear in the incoming list with a status of **Pending**.
3. For each question, you have two choices:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Approve** | Makes the question visible to the full audience on their screens |
| **Hide** (or dismiss) | Removes it from the visible list — only you see it |

[SHOW: the Q&A list with questions in Pending state]
[NARRATE: "The profanity filter (configured in the Settings sub-tab) automatically blocks inappropriate words. But you are the second filter — review each question before approving it. You can see the question, the submitter's name if they provided one, and the time it came in."]

4. Approve questions you want the room to see and discuss.
5. Hide questions that are off-topic, inappropriate, or already answered.

---

### Step 6 — Sending Announcements

Announcements push a text message to all attendees on the event page instantly.

1. Click the **Announce** sub-tab.
2. Type your announcement in the text field — for example:
   - "Session starting in 5 minutes — please take your seats."
   - "The breakout session is now in Room C, not Room B."
   - "Wi-Fi password: SUMMIT2026"
3. Click **Send** or **Post Announcement**.
4. The message appears on the audience's screens immediately.

[SHOW: the announcement field with text typed and the Send button]
[NARRATE: "Announcements are a direct broadcast. There is no push notification here — it appears on the event page for anyone who has it open. Use it for logistical updates, schedule changes, or a quick message to the room."]

---

### Step 7 — Word Cloud

The Word Cloud lets the audience type one or a few words in response to a prompt — for example, "What is your biggest leadership challenge?" — and the result is a live word cloud visualization.

1. Click the **Word Cloud** sub-tab.
2. Type a prompt or question to show the audience.
3. Click the button to activate the Word Cloud.
4. As the audience submits words, the cloud builds in real time.
5. Common words grow larger.
6. Use this for icebreakers, reflection moments, or to visualize group thinking.

[SHOW: the Word Cloud with audience submissions building]
[NARRATE: "Word Clouds are a great opener or mid-session engagement tool. Ask one good question and let the room respond. The visual builds while you watch."]

**To reset the Word Cloud:**
- Click the **Clear** or **Reset** button to wipe submissions and start fresh for a new question.

---

### Step 8 — Turn the event OFF at the end

When the event is over:

1. Click the back arrow to return to the event list.
2. Find the live toggle on your event's card.
3. Click the toggle to turn it off (it will return to grey).
4. The LIVE badge will disappear.
5. The engagement features will no longer be visible to the audience.

[SHOW: the toggle turning grey — LIVE badge disappearing]
[NARRATE: "Turn the event off as soon as you are done. This prevents stale polls or Q&A from being visible to anyone who loads the event page later."]

---

### Step 9 — Export engagement data after the event

After the event, you can review and export the data from your polls.

1. Click into the event from the Conference tab.
2. Click the **Polls** sub-tab.
3. Look for a **Download** or **Export** button on any completed poll.
4. Click it to download the poll results as a data file.

[SHOW: the export button on a completed poll]
[NARRATE: "Poll results are valuable data. Export them after every event and keep them on file — they tell you what your audience was thinking and what topics resonated."]

## Quick Checklist: Before the Event Starts

Use this checklist before you flip the event live:

- [ ] Event is published in the Events tab
- [ ] Sessions are saved in the Conference tab
- [ ] At least one poll is created and ready to deploy
- [ ] Profanity filter is configured (Settings sub-tab)
- [ ] You have the admin dashboard open on your device
- [ ] You have tested the audience view on a second device or browser

---

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: The audience says they cannot see the Q&A or polls.**
Fix: Confirm the event live toggle is ON (green). If the toggle is off, no engagement features appear to the audience.

**Problem: A poll I deployed is not showing on my screen.**
Fix: Try refreshing the Polls sub-tab. If the poll shows as deployed but the results are not updating, confirm the audience is on the correct event page URL.

**Problem: Inappropriate questions are getting through the filter.**
Fix: Go to the Settings sub-tab and click Profanity Filter. Add the specific word or phrase to the blocked list and save. New submissions will be filtered; past submissions must be hidden manually.

**Problem: I turned the event off but the audience still sees the engagement page.**
Fix: The event page may be cached in some browsers. The engagement tools (polls, Q&A boxes) will stop accepting new submissions once the event is off, even if the page is still visible. The audience must refresh to see the page as it appears post-event.
