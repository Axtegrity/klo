# Tutorial 01: Getting Into the Admin Dashboard
**Duration estimate:** 5 minutes
**Series:** KLO Admin Video Tutorials

---

## Prerequisites

Before you start this tutorial, you need:
- Your admin email address and password (provided by your developer)
- A web browser (Chrome or Safari recommended)
- The site URL: **keithlodom.ai**

---

## What You Will Learn

By the end of this video, you will know how to sign in, find the admin dashboard, read the 15 tabs across the top, and use the four header utility buttons.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1 — Open the website

1. Open your web browser.
2. In the address bar at the top, type: **keithlodom.ai** and press Enter.

### Step 2 — Sign in

1. Look at the navigation bar at the top of the page.
2. Click the **Sign In** button.

[SHOW: top navigation bar with Sign In visible]

3. You will be taken to a sign-in form.
4. In the **Email** field, type your admin email address.

[SHOW: the sign-in form with the email and password fields]

### Step 3 — Find the Admin link

1. After signing in, look back at the navigation bar at the top of the page.
2. You will see a gold link that says **Admin**.
3. Click **Admin**.

[SHOW: the navigation bar with the gold Admin link highlighted]
[NARRATE: "That gold Admin link only appears for admin and owner accounts. If you do not see it, your account may not have the right role — contact your developer."]

### Step 4 — Read the dashboard layout

You are now inside the Admin Dashboard. Take a moment to look at the screen.

At the very top, you will see a heading that says **Admin Dashboard**, with a short description underneath.

In the top-right corner, there are four buttons. Remember these — you will use them often:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Changelog** | Shows a history of every update made to the site |
| **Request Update** | Lets you submit a change request to your developer |
| **Training Guide** | Opens the built-in step-by-step reference guide |
| **Refresh** | Reloads all the data on the dashboard |

[SHOW: the four header buttons in the top-right corner]
[NARRATE: "Think of Request Update as your direct line to your developer. Instead of texting or emailing, you fill in a form right here and they get it immediately."]
[PAUSE: let them read the four buttons]

### Step 5 — Learn the tabs

Below the header, you will see a row of tab buttons. These are the main areas of your admin dashboard. Scroll the row left and right if all the tabs are not visible at once.

The tabs are, in order:

1. **Overview** — Your main stats at a glance (users, revenue, AI usage)
2. **Creative Studio** — Upload media, set hero images, manage themes
3. **Customize** — Brand colors and which features are active
4. **Content** — Edit the words and text across the site
5. **Testimonials** — Manage member testimonials
6. **Surveys** — Create and publish multi-question surveys
7. **Events** — Create and manage events with files and countdowns
8. **Conference** — Live event tools: polls, Q&A, announcements
9. **Inquiries** — Booking and consultation requests from the website
10. **Notifications** — Send push notifications to your members
11. **Presentations** — Upload and publish presentation materials
12. **Users** — See and manage all registered accounts
13. **Analytics** (labeled "Content" in the tab bar) — Assessment scores and vault usage
14. **Revenue** — Subscription and payment tracking
15. **Tools** — Advanced settings; handle with care

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: I do not see the gold Admin link after signing in.**
Fix: Your account may have the wrong role. Contact your developer. Only accounts with the "admin" or "owner" role see the Admin link.

**Problem: The sign-in page says my email or password is wrong.**
Fix: Double-check for any extra spaces before or after your email address. Passwords are case-sensitive. If you are still locked out, contact your developer to reset your credentials.

**Problem: The tabs are cut off and I cannot see all of them.**
Fix: The tab row scrolls horizontally. On a computer, click and drag the row sideways. On a phone or tablet, swipe the row left with your finger.
