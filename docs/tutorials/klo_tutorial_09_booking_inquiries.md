## How Inquiries Work

When a visitor fills out the **Book Keith** or **Request a Consultation** form on the website, their submission goes directly to the Inquiries tab in your admin dashboard. You see every detail they provided — contact information, event type, budget, timeline, and their message.

There are two types of inquiries:
- **Booking** — speaking engagement requests (event name, audience size, budget)
- **Consultation** — one-on-one advisory requests (area of interest, current challenges)

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1 — Open the Inquiries tab

1. Click the **Inquiries** tab in the tab row at the top of the admin dashboard.

### Step 2 — Read the filters at the top

At the top of the Inquiries tab, you will see a filter bar with four controls:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Search box** | Filter by name or email |
| **All Types dropdown** | Filter by Booking or Consultation |
| **All Statuses dropdown** | Filter by New, Reviewed, Contacted, or Archived |
| **Refresh button** | Reload the list from the database |

Below the filter bar, the total inquiry count is shown — for example, "14 inquiries found."

[SHOW: the filter bar with all four controls labeled]

---

### Step 3 — Review new inquiries first

Start every session by looking at your new inquiries.

1. Click the **All Statuses** dropdown.
2. Select **New**.
3. The list now shows only inquiries you have not yet reviewed.

[SHOW: the status dropdown open with New selected]
[NARRATE: "New means the inquiry came in and no one has touched it yet. Make it a habit to check the Inquiries tab every morning and clear out the New queue."]

---

### Step 4 — Read an inquiry

1. Click anywhere on an inquiry row.

A full-detail panel will slide open (or a modal window will appear) showing everything the person submitted.

What you will see for a **Booking** inquiry:
- Name, email, phone, organization
- Event name and date
- Event type (keynote, workshop, panel, etc.)
- Audience size
- Budget range
- Location
- Additional details and message

What you will see for a **Consultation** inquiry:
- Name, email, phone, organization
- Area of interest
- Organization size
- Industry
- Current challenge
- Timeline
- Whether they have worked with a consultant before
- Message

### Step 5 — Update the inquiry status

After reading an inquiry, update its status to reflect where you are in the process.

1. Inside the inquiry detail panel, look for the **Update Status** dropdown.
2. Click it and choose the appropriate status:

| Status | When to use it |
|---|---|
| **New** | Just arrived — not yet reviewed |
| **Reviewed** | You have read it and are deciding next steps |
| **Contacted** | You have reached out to this person |
| **Archived** | This inquiry is closed — either completed, declined, or no longer relevant |

3. The status updates immediately when you select it — no separate save button needed.

[SHOW: the Update Status dropdown being used to change a status from New to Reviewed]
[NARRATE: "Keeping statuses accurate is how you stay organized when multiple people or a team member might also check the Inquiries tab. Anyone who looks can see exactly where things stand."]

---

### Step 6 — Close the detail panel

1. Click the **X** button in the top-right corner of the detail panel to close it.
2. You return to the inquiry list.

---

### Step 7 — Work through the list systematically

A good weekly workflow:

1. Filter by **New** — read and update status on each one.
2. Filter by **Reviewed** — follow up on any you were deciding about.
3. Filter by **Contacted** — check if replies have come in, archive the ones that are resolved.

[SHOW: switching between the status filters]

---

### Step 8 — Search for a specific person

If Keith or a team member mentions a specific person's name and you need to find their inquiry:

1. Click in the search box.
2. Type the person's name or email address.
3. The list filters as you type.
4. Click on the matching row to view the full details.

[SHOW: the search box being used with a name typed]

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: I see the number badge on the Inquiries tab but when I open it, the list shows nothing.**
Fix: Check the filter settings. If the status filter is set to something other than "all" from a previous session, new inquiries may be filtered out. Reset all filters to "all" to see everything.

**Problem: A status change I made is not showing after I close and reopen the inquiry.**
Fix: Status changes save instantly in the database. If the display seems stale, click the **Refresh** button in the filter bar to reload the list.

**Problem: I cannot find an inquiry I know came in.**
Fix: First try clearing the search box and resetting all filters. If the inquiry is more than a few weeks old, it may have been archived by someone else. Set the status filter to Archived to check.

**Problem: The inquiry list is loading slowly.**
Fix: Click the Refresh button. If it continues to be slow, check your internet connection. The inquiry list loads from the database each time you open the tab.
